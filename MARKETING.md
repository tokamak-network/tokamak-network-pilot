## 2. The Story of the Forest

### Origin Story — "The Clearing"

*Use this narrative on the About page, in pitch decks, in long-form social posts, and as the emotional backbone of the brand.*

---

Imagine you arrive at the edge of a vast, ancient forest.

You know it holds everything you need — answers about how things work, where things are, why decisions were made. Somewhere in those trees is the exact contract you're looking for. Somewhere beneath the canopy is the explanation of how staking works, how the sequencer processes transactions, how Titan was built.

But the forest is dense. The paths aren't marked. Documentation lives in one grove, smart contract code in another, governance discussions scattered like fallen leaves across dozens of clearings. You could spend hours — days — navigating between repositories, README files, outdated wikis, and buried GitHub issues, hoping to stumble upon the answer you need.

**That's what building on Tokamak Network felt like.**

Not because the knowledge didn't exist. It did. Hundreds of repositories, thousands of files, years of engineering. The ecosystem was rich — but the knowledge was scattered across the forest floor with no map, no guide, no trail.

**So we planted a seed.**

We built Tokamak Forest: an AI that reads every repository, every document, every piece of code across the Tokamak Network ecosystem. It doesn't just store them — it *understands* them. It breaks them into meaning. It connects ideas across projects, across time, across languages.

Now, when you step into the Forest, you don't need to know where the answer lives. You just ask.

*"How does TON staking delegation work?"*

And the Forest answers. Not with a guess. Not with a hallucination. With a sourced, cited answer that points you to the exact file, the exact line, the exact document where the truth lives.

**The scattered knowledge found its roots. The forest grew a canopy. And now, anyone can walk in and find their way.**

---

### The Deeper Metaphor — Why "Forest"?

A forest is not a library. A library is organized, rigid, static — someone decided where every book goes. A forest is *alive*. It grows. New trees sprout. Old branches fall. The root systems underground connect trees you'd never think were related.

That's exactly how knowledge works in a living ecosystem like Tokamak Network:

- New repositories appear weekly
- Documentation gets updated, moved, deprecated
- Code in one project silently depends on decisions made in another
- The real understanding isn't in any single file — it's in the *connections between them*

Tokamak Forest mirrors this. It doesn't just index files — it finds the hidden connections. It understands that a question about Titan's bridge is related to a contract in `tokamak-network/contracts-v2` and a design doc buried in a GitHub issue from two years ago. It weaves them together into a single, coherent answer.

A forest, not a filing cabinet.

## 4. Logo Concept

### Primary Logo Mark

**Concept: "The Knowledge Tree"**

A stylized tree where:
- The **trunk** subtly incorporates the shape of a tokamak reactor cross-section (a donut/torus shape) — connecting to the Tokamak brand
- The **branches** extend outward like neural network connections or knowledge graph edges
- The **leaves/canopy** are represented as small glowing dots (like data points or embeddings) connected by thin lines
- The overall silhouette reads as a **tree** from a distance but reveals the **network/AI** metaphor up close

**Style:** Minimal, geometric, modern. Not overly detailed — should work as a favicon (16x16) and a large banner.

### Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Forest Black** | `#0A1A0F` | Primary background, dark UI |
| **Deep Green** | `#064E3B` | Secondary backgrounds, cards |

| **Sage** | `#6EE7B7` | Secondary accent, hover states, highlights |

### Logo Variations

| Variant | Use Case |
|---------|----------|
| **Full Logo** (icon + "Tokamak Forest" wordmark) | Website header, documentation, presentations |
| **Icon Only** (tree mark) | Favicon, app icon, social media profile picture, small placements |
| **Wordmark Only** ("Tokamak Forest" text) | When the icon is already present nearby |
| **Monochrome White** | Dark backgrounds, overlays |
| **Monochrome Black** | Light backgrounds, print |

### Logo Don'ts

- Don't stretch or distort
- Don't place on busy/clashing backgrounds without a container
- Don't change the colors outside the brand palette
- Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "T" in "Tokamak" on all sides

- Use active voice: "Tokamak Forest answers your questions" not "Questions are answered by Tokamak Forest"
